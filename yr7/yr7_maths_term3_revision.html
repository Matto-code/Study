The 10% method — for any percentage, in your head

Find 10% first (divide by 10 — just move the decimal point). Then build up or down from there.
Find 35% of $140 — without a calculator ▼
1
10% of 140 = 14 (move the decimal point one place)
2
30% = 3 × 14 = 42
3
5% = half of 10% = 7
4
35% = 30% + 5% = 42 + 7
✓ $49
This is much faster than multiplying by 0.35 by hand — always build percentages from 10%, 5% and 1%.
Find 17.5% of $80 — without a calculator (common "tricky" percentage) ▼
1
10% of 80 = 8
2
5% = half of 10% = 4
3
2.5% = half of 5% = 2
4
17.5% = 10% + 5% + 2.5% = 8 + 4 + 2
✓ $14

Multiplying & dividing decimals by hand

Multiply: ignore the decimal points, multiply as whole numbers, then count the total decimal places and place the point back in.
Divide: multiply both numbers by 10 (or 100) until the number you're dividing by is a whole number — then divide as normal.
Petrol costs $1.85/L. Find the cost of 32.5 L — long multiplication, no calculator ▼
1
Ignore decimals, multiply whole numbers: 185 × 325
2
Break it up: 185 × 300 = 55,500
3
Then: 185 × 25 = 4,625
4
Add: 55,500 + 4,625 = 60,125
5
Count decimal places (2 + 1 = 3) and place the point: 60.125
✓ $60.13 (rounded to the nearest cent)
Breaking a big multiplication into friendly chunks (like ×300 then ×25) is much easier to do in your head or on paper than one big sum.

Improper fractions & mixed numerals

Improper → mixed: divide numerator by denominator. Whole number = the answer, remainder goes over the same denominator.
Mixed → improper: multiply the whole number by the denominator, add the numerator, keep the same denominator.
Convert 23/6 to a mixed numeral. ▼
1
Divide: 23 ÷ 6 = 3 remainder 5
2
Remainder goes over the denominator: 3⅚
✓ 23/6 = 3⅚
Convert 4¾ to an improper fraction. ▼
1
Multiply whole number by denominator: 4 × 4 = 16
2
Add the numerator: 16 + 3 = 19
✓ 4¾ = 19/4

Equivalent fractions & ordering

Multiply or divide the numerator and denominator by the same number to make an equivalent fraction.
To compare fractions, convert them to a common denominator first (or convert both to decimals).
Which is larger: 5/6 or 7/9? ▼
1
Common denominator is 18: 5/6 = 15/18 and 7/9 = 14/18
2
Compare: 15/18 > 14/18
✓ 5/6 is larger

Multiply: multiply numerators together and denominators together. Divide: flip the second fraction (reciprocal), then multiply.

WRITTEN: A recipe needs 2¾ cups of flour for 12 muffins. How much for 18 muffins? ▼
1
Scale factor: 18 ÷ 12 = 1.5
2
Multiply: 2.75 × 1.5 = 4.125
✓ 4⅛ cups (4.125 cups)
Scaling a recipe works the same way as scaling any ratio — find the multiplier first.
WRITTEN: 40% of a class of 30 play sport. 1/4 of those also play an instrument. How many play both? ▼
1
40% of 30: 0.4 × 30 = 12
2
1/4 of 12: 12 ÷ 4 = 3
✓ 3 students

Conventions & substituting

3n means 3 × n. A number next to a letter is always multiplied — no × sign needed. Write the number first: 5x, not x5.
Substituting: replace each letter with its given value, then calculate following order of operations.

Like terms, multiplying & dividing

Only terms with the exact same letter(s) and power can be combined. 3a × 2b = 6ab. 8ab ÷ 2a = 4b.
Simplify 7x + 4y − 3x − 2y + 5 ▼
1
Group x terms: 7x − 3x = 4x
2
Group y terms: 4y − 2y = 2y
✓ 4x + 2y + 5
Simplify 15xy ÷ 5x ▼
1
Divide numbers: 15 ÷ 5 = 3
2
Cancel the common letter x, leaving y
✓ 3y

Expanding brackets

Multiply the term outside the bracket by every term inside it.
Expand 5(2x − 3) ▼
1
5 × 2x = 10x
2
5 × (−3) = −15
✓ 10x − 15
Expand x(x + 6) ▼
1
x × x = x²
2
x × 6 = 6x
✓ x² + 6x
WRITTEN: P = 2(l + w). Find P when l=9, w=4. Then find a general expression for length (x+2), width x. ▼
1
P = 2(9 + 4) = 2(13) = 26 cm
2
General: P = 2((x+2) + x) = 2(2x + 2)
✓ P = 26 cm; general P = 4x + 4
WRITTEN: A plumber charges $50 call-out plus $35/hour. Write an expression for cost, find cost for 3 hours. ▼
1
Expression: C = 50 + 35h
2
For h=3: 50 + 35(3) = 50 + 105
✓ C = 50 + 35h; cost = $155

One-step & two-step equations

Do the opposite (inverse) operation to both sides to isolate the letter. For two-step equations, undo addition/subtraction first, then multiplication/division.
Solve x/4 = 9 ▼
1
Multiply both sides by 4: x = 9 × 4
✓ x = 36
Solve 6x + 7 = 37 ▼
1
Subtract 7: 6x = 30
2
Divide by 6: x = 5
✓ x = 5
Solve x/3 − 2 = 5 ▼
1
Add 2: x/3 = 7
2
Multiply by 3: x = 21
✓ x = 21

Substituting into formulas

Replace each variable in the formula with its given value, then calculate.
Use v = u + at to find v when u=5, a=2, t=4. ▼
1
v = 5 + (2 × 4) = 5 + 8
✓ v = 13
Use A = ½bh to find A when b=10, h=6. ▼
1
A = ½ × 10 × 6 = ½ × 60
✓ A = 30

Forming & solving equations

Turn the words into an equation using a letter for the unknown, then solve it.
'Seven less than three times a number is 20.' Find the number. ▼
1
Form the equation: 3x − 7 = 20
2
Add 7: 3x = 27. Divide by 3: x = 9
✓ x = 9
WRITTEN: Sarah is 4 years older than twice her brother's age. Sarah is 20. Find her brother's age. ▼
1
Let b = brother's age: 2b + 4 = 20
2
Subtract 4: 2b = 16. Divide by 2: b = 8
✓ Brother is 8 years old
WRITTEN: F = (9/5)C + 32. If F = 77°F, form an equation and solve for C. ▼
1
77 = (9/5)C + 32
2
Subtract 32: 45 = (9/5)C. Multiply by 5/9: C = 25
✓ C = 25°C

Place value & rounding

Digits after the decimal point are tenths, hundredths, thousandths. To round, look at the digit after the place you're rounding to: 5 or more rounds up.
Round 8.0956 to 2 decimal places. ▼
1
Third decimal digit is 5, which rounds up the hundredths digit (9 → 10, carries over)
✓ 8.10

Worked examples

Share $90 in the ratio 4:5 ▼
1
Total parts: 4 + 5 = 9
2
One part: 90 ÷ 9 = $10
3
Shares: 4×10=$40 and 5×10=$50
✓ $40 : $50
A school has boys:girls in ratio 3:4. There are 21 boys. How many girls? ▼
1
3 parts = 21, so 1 part = 21 ÷ 3 = 7
2
Girls = 4 parts = 4 × 7 = 28
✓ 28 girls
WRITTEN: Cordial and water mixed 1:4. Make 2.5 L in total — how much of each? ▼
1
Total parts: 1 + 4 = 5
2
One part: 2.5 ÷ 5 = 0.5 L
✓ Cordial = 0.5 L, Water = 2 L
WRITTEN: Blue:white paint mixed 3:7. 14 L of white is used. Find blue paint and total volume. ▼
1
White = 7 parts = 14 L, so 1 part = 14 ÷ 7 = 2 L
2
Blue = 3 parts = 3 × 2 = 6 L. Total parts = 10 → 10 × 2 = 20 L
✓ Blue = 6 L; Total = 20 L
